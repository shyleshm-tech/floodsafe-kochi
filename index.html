🎓 How FloodSafe works

Step 1: The emergency user selects their location directly on the map.

Step 2: The user enters the emergency destination.

Step 3: The routing service provides available road alternatives.

Step 4: The system considers both road distance and simulated flood risk.

Route Cost = Distance + Flood Risk Penalty

Step 5: The route with the lowest combined cost is highlighted as the proposed safest route.

⚠️ Demo limitation: Flood-risk values in this prototype are simulated. A real emergency system should use live flood depth, road-closure, rainfall, river-level, and traffic data.
